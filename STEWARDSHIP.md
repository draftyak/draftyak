# Stewardship

DraftYak's core is Apache-2.0. These promises govern the project and its commercial offerings.

- Free features never move to paid tiers.
- Core has no licence keys, plan checks or telemetry.
- No DraftYak offering, including core, Cloud or Team, shows ads or sells data.
- Every extension point has a free implementation that ships no later than its commercial counterpart.
- Contributions use DCO 1.1, with no CLA and no additional relicensing rights. Apache-2.0 inbound equals outbound.
- All core tests are public.
- Third-party plugins are never repossessed by the project.

## Free implementations before paid services

The ACME DNS-01 CertProvider and the Apache-2.0 relay server and connector ship with or before the hosted relay. UnifiedPush/ntfy support ships with or before paid push. The relay follows the MVP; this promise does not announce a service availability date.

Slack features are never gated behind payment. Slack data classes stay out of paid backups and push content. Commercial offerings must preserve the [privacy commitments](PRIVACY.md) and the per-item desktop approval boundary in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Accountability

Product and policy changes are reviewed through [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Raise a concern about these promises in [Discussions](https://github.com/draftyak/draftyak/discussions); report a vulnerability privately under SECURITY.md. The code licence remains [Apache-2.0](LICENSE); the [trademark policy](TRADEMARKS.md) covers the name separately.
