# DraftYak trademark policy

Nick Savov (individual) owns the DraftYak™ mark. The [Apache-2.0 licence](LICENSE) grants rights to the code, not trademark rights. Copyright notices, including `Copyright 2026 The DraftYak Authors`, remain subject to the licence and must be preserved as required.

This policy follows the permission-based approach of the [Model Trademark Guidelines, version 1.0 (FSF-hosted copy)](https://static.fsf.org/nosvn/licensing/2020/ModelTrademarkGuidelinesv1.0.pdf) (read 2026-10-18), with the DraftYak-specific rules below. It does not claim that the mark is registered or cleared in any jurisdiction.

## Uses permitted without separate permission

- Use DraftYak in plain text to identify, discuss, review or accurately describe compatibility with the project. Do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
- Use the form “<Name> for DraftYak” as a descriptive integration or plugin title, with your own name prominent and a clear statement of independence. This does not permit a domain or service name containing the mark.
- Redistribute unmodified DraftYak releases under the DraftYak name, preserving required notices and identifying your distribution source accurately. Do not present your distribution as the project's official distribution.

## Modified builds and reserved names

Modified builds must rebrand: use a distinct product name and branding. You may explain that the build is based on DraftYak and retain the notices the code licence requires. Rebranding does not require removing accurate source references or legal notices.

Do not use “draftyak”, regardless of case, in a domain or service name without written permission from the trademark owner. “DraftYak Cloud”, “DraftYak Team” and “Official” are reserved for use authorized by the owner; do not use them to suggest that an independent product or service is official.

Do not imply that the owner endorses your product, service or organization. These permissions do not grant rights to vendor marks. References to vendors in DraftYak materials use their names only as plain text; their marks remain subject to their owners' rules.
